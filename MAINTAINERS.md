# Maintainers

[MatData](https://matdata.eu) maintains this project; [@MathiasVDA](https://github.com/MathiasVDA)
is the lead maintainer.

Scope: a browser app that edits RDF data through forms generated from SHACL/DASH shapes,
against any SPARQL 1.1 endpoint. Authentication, reasoning and ontology editing are out of
scope and belong in other tools.

Decisions that shape the project are recorded in [docs/adr/](docs/adr/).
